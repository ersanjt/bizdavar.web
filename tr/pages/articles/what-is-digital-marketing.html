Dijital pazarlama, doğru kitleye çevrimiçi ulaşmaktır the right audience online — site, search, social, ads and email — to build awareness, trust and sales. Bizdavar Group bünyesinde it is not “posting”; it is funnel, measurement and digital marketing services.

A working definition

If a campaign brings visits but WhatsApp stays quiet, marketing is incomplete. We measure cost per lead and conversion, not likes.

Main channels

  • SEO and content — including this blog cluster.
  • Search ads — controlled spend on buyer-intent queries.
  • SMM — see the SMM guide.
  • Website — start fast with Fast Web Studio.

Vs traditional

You can move budget weekly. Technical primer: SEO Starter Guide.

Where to start

  1. One clear service page with WhatsApp/form.
  2. Two or three cluster articles linking to it.
  3. Test ads only after conversion tracking.
