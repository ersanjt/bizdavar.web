The basics

What are UTM parameters?

A tag on your link

UTM adds a few parameters to the end of a URL. The page stays the same, but your analytics tool learns where the visitor came from.

Three key parameters

source says where (instagram), medium says what kind of channel (social) and campaign says which campaign (black_friday). term and content are optional.

Reports in Google Analytics

In GA4, open Reports › Acquisition › Traffic acquisition to see visits and sales for each source, medium and campaign.

UTM naming rules

  1. Always use lowercase — Instagram and instagram show up as two different sources.
  2. Use dashes or underscores instead of spaces and keep names short.
  3. Don't add UTMs to internal links on your own site; it overwrites the real session source.
  4. Keep campaign names in a shared sheet so the whole team names things the same way.
FAQ

UTM questions

What is a UTM link?

A link with utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign parameters added so tools like Google Analytics record exactly where a visit came from.

Do UTM parameters hurt SEO?

No, as long as the page has a correct canonical tag — Google indexes the version without UTMs. Just don't use UTMs on internal links.

Where do I see UTM results?

In GA4 go to Reports › Acquisition › Traffic acquisition and choose the Session source / medium or Session campaign dimension.

Do I need UTMs for Google Ads?

Not if Google Ads is linked to GA4 with auto-tagging on. For Instagram, email, WhatsApp and other ads, always add UTMs.

My UTM link is too long — what can I do?

Shorten it with any link shortener. The parameters are kept when the short link opens.
