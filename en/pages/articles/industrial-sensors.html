Picking level, pressure or point-level sensors is not brochure shopping: media, temperature, process connection and certificates decide the model. Bizdavar supplies VEGA and, for gas/flame, Prosense with a clear proforma.

Measurement type

  • Radar (VEGAPULS) — non-contact level.
  • Pressure (VEGABAR) — process pressure / hydrostatic level.
  • Switch (VEGAPOINT) — compact point level.
