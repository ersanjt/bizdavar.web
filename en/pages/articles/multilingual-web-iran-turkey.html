A business serving Persian and Turkish customers fails with one language or machine translation without hreflang. Bizdavar ships this pattern in projects and Fast Web Studio.

Why two languages

Local trust: WhatsApp numbers, RTL, separate currency. Persian service pages must not be broken Turkish clones.

hreflang and indexing

Separate URLs, per-locale canonicals, reciprocal hreflang. See Google localized versions. This site’s blog uses fa/tr/en/ru/ar.

Local trust

  • Portfolio
  • Bilingual contact
  • Cluster articles instead of one URL for all languages

FAQ

Will Google mix language versions?
Not if hreflang and canonical are correct. See Localized versions.
Does Fast Studio ship multilingual?
Yes; fa/tr/en structure can be scoped in Fast Web Studio.
