MarviSpace — leather goods e-commerce store

Custom programming, development and storefront design for a leather products brand — by Bizdavar Group

About the project

MarviSpace is a Bizdavar Group e-commerce programming and design case study — a custom leather goods store with a minimal catalog, checkout flow and admin dashboard. This page is a custom e-commerce web design and development case study.

Project challenge

A leather brand needed a custom store — not a ready-made theme: minimal product grid, SKU codes, an international shopping experience and a content admin panel.

Results

  • Live leather catalog on marvispace.com
  • Minimal shopping experience with a custom visual identity
  • Product and content admin panel for the sales team

Client field of activity

MarviSpace is an online leather goods brand — jackets, apparel and accessories. Product and order details are published on the client’s official website.
