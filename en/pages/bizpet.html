About the product

BizPet (Biz Pet / Pet Biz) is the Padideh heating pad — for warming in cold seasons and localized pain relief, designed and produced at the University of Tabriz Technology Incubator.

It was developed with Technology Pardazan Tosee Khavarmianeh and the technology unit Imen Sahand Padideh. The pad is portable for home or car use.
