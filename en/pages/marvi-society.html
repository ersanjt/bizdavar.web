Marvi Society — invite-only iOS club for Istanbul

Zero-to-launch iOS design and development — connecting verified creators with Istanbul venues; live on the App Store

About the project

Marvi Society is a Bizdavar Group mobile case study — an invite-only iOS app that connects verified creators with approved Istanbul venues. Discover events and places, request collaborations, check in, submit visit proof, manage venues and TR/EN profiles. This page is an iOS app case study.

Project challenge

An invite-only app to connect creators and venues in Istanbul — with event discovery, invite management, check-in and visit proof, ready for App Store release.

Services delivered by Bizdavar

  • طراحی محصول و UI/UX اپ iOS
  • برنامه‌نویسی و معماری اپ موبایل
  • جریان کشف، دعوت، check-in و مدرک بازدید
  • پنل مدیریت مکان، کمپین و اعلان‌ها
  • انتشار در App Store و پشتیبانی پس از لانچ

Results

  • iOS app live on the App Store
  • End-to-end discover, collab and venue management flow
  • Dark visual identity with TR/EN experience

Product field of activity

Marvi Society is an invite-only lifestyle club in Istanbul — matching creators with restaurants, nightlife, wellness and beauty venues. Product details are published on the website and App Store page.
