Web design case study

ASIL — Website, server and company email

Dubai horse and livestock feed brand — bilingual website, server setup and company email by Bizdavar Group

About the project

ASIL is a Bizdavar Group client — the equine and livestock nutrition brand of Bio Balance International Foodstuff Trading LLC in Dubai. We designed the bilingual website and set up the server and company email on asilfeed.com. This page is a web design and infrastructure case study.

Project challenge

The Dubai brand needed a bilingual website, a stable server and company email on its own domain so stables and regional buyers could see products and contact paths in one place.

Services delivered by Bizdavar

  • Bilingual corporate website design and development (English and Arabic)
  • Product catalog, feed finder and contact pages
  • Server setup and hosting for asilfeed.com
  • Company email mailboxes on the domain
  • Mobile layout and content structure

Results

  • Live site at asilfeed.com covering feeds, supplements and livestock
  • Server provisioned and the site running on company infrastructure
  • Company email live, including info@asilfeed.com

Client field of activity

ASIL supplies horse feeds, vitamins and supplements, and feed for camels, sheep and goats, formulated for Gulf conditions. Product details are published on the client’s official website.
