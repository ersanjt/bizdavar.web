BizTab — Non-contact wall thermometer

First non-contact wall thermometer designed in Tabriz — developed by the Bizdavar team with measurement error under 0.3°C

About the product

BizTab is a non-contact wall thermometer designed and developed by the Bizdavar team. Introduced as the first non-contact wall thermometer in Tabriz, all intellectual property and exclusive production rights belong to the Bizdavar team.

Approved by University of Tabriz professors, it is designed for wall mounting at building entrances, healthcare facilities and high-traffic areas — contactless measurement with a digital temperature display.

Technical features

  • اندازه‌گیری غیرتماسی (infrared)
  • خطای اندازه‌گیری کمتر از ۰.۳ درجه سانتی‌گراد
  • نمایشگر دیجیتال LED
  • نصب دیواری — مناسب ورودی و سالن
  • تأیید علمی اساتید دانشگاه تبریز
  • تولید انحصاری تیم بیزدوار
