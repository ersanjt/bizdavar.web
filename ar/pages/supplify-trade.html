Supplify Trade — International trade website

UAE (RAKEZ) B2B client project — corporate web design, SEO and digital support by Bizdavar Group

About the project

Supplify Trade FZ-LLC is a Bizdavar Group client — a RAKEZ-licensed international trade and sourcing company in Ras Al Khaimah, UAE. We designed and implemented the corporate website, content structure, foundational SEO and technical support. This page is a web design case study.

Services delivered by Bizdavar

  • طراحی و توسعه وبسایت شرکتی B2B
  • هویت بصری و رابط کاربری چندبخشی (خدمات، صنایع، فرآیند، تماس)
  • سئو پایه، ساختار محتوا و صفحات تبدیل (RFQ)
  • بهینه‌سازی موبایل و عملکرد
  • پشتیبانی فنی، به‌روزرسانی و نگهداری سایت

Client field of activity

Supplify Trade FZ-LLC operates in international sourcing, procurement, quality control and logistics — construction materials, industrial goods, agricultural equipment and automotive spare parts. Service and quotation details are published on the client’s official website.
