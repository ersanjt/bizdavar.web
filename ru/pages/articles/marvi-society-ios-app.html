Marvi Society is an invite-only creator and venue club in Istanbul — native iOS with events, discovery and profiles. Bizdavar delivered UI/UX, Swift development and App Store release.

Project brief

The brand needed a native app, not a PWA: wireframes → UI kit → Swift → TestFlight → App Store. Screenshots and download link on the case study page and portfolio.

Stack

  • Swift (UIKit/SwiftUI)
  • Events and creator profile APIs
  • Push and invite deep links

Key features

Venue discovery, RSVP, creator profiles and invite flow. Similar project: app services or contact. Related: Fast Studio (web) and multilingual web.
