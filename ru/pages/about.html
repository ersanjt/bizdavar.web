What is Bizdavar Group?

Bizdavar Group is a digital agency and B2B supply group founded in 2013 by Ersan Jahed Tabrizi. Our digital services include digital marketing, web design, fintech platforms and SMM management. Owned products and industrial brands live on the products page.

100+ custom web and platform projects across 11 countries — from custom web and platforms to VEGA supply and digital products. Digital infrastructure, domains, hosting, SSL and technical support are also managed by our team. View portfolio
