SMM Turk — SMM panel and website design

Turkey client project — panel UI, website and digital support by Bizdavar Group

About the project

SMM Turk is a Bizdavar Group client — an SMM panel platform for social growth with competitive pricing, 24/7 automated delivery and affiliate / reseller programs. We designed and implemented the website and panel experience, content structure, foundational SEO and technical support. This page is a web design case study.

Project challenge

A Turkey SMM panel needed clear UI for signup, dashboard, and conversion pages — with mobile focus and signup rate in mind.
