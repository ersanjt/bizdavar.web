BizClean V5 — Automatic hand sanitizer

Automatic device with ultrasonic sensor — built during the global COVID-19 pandemic, engineered by the Bizdavar team

About the product

BizClean V5 is an automatic hand sanitizer engineered by the Bizdavar team — for building entrances, offices, stores and healthcare facilities.

Developed during the COVID-19 pandemic; from Dey 1398 to Shahrivar 1401 it was personally funded by the founder and manufactured by the Bizdavar team. Showcased at the 1399 growth center exhibition (ISNA, SNN, IRIB) and at RINOTEX 1400 via the «Imen Sahand Padideh» science park unit; now available on Digikala.
