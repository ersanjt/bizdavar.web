Privacy Policy

How your information is collected and used on the Bizdavar Group website

1. Information we collect

When you submit the contact form, we receive your name, email, phone number (optional), service type and message. This information is used only to respond to your request.

2. How we use it

  • پاسخ به درخواست مشاوره و پیشنهاد خدمات
  • پیگیری پروژه‌های در حال مذاکره
  • بهبود کیفیت خدمات (بدون انتشار عمومی)

3. Sharing with third parties

Your information is not sold or transferred to third parties without your explicit consent, except where legally required.

4. Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Tag Manager (GTM-NXWQQWF8) and Google Analytics 4 (G-4GFEY12SLH) to measure traffic and improve the experience. These tools may set cookies or similar identifiers. Language detection details are in section 5.

5. Language & region detection

To show the appropriate language (Persian, Turkish, English), your IP address may be sent anonymously to ipapi.co or ipwho.is to detect your approximate country. Your language preference is stored in browser localStorage (key bizdavar_locale). You can change language anytime from the menu or with ?lang=fa, ?lang=tr or ?lang=en.

5. Language and region detection

To show the right language (Persian, Turkish, English, Russian, Arabic), your IP may be sent anonymously to ipapi.co or ipwho.is for approximate country detection. Language preference is stored in browser localStorage (bizdavar_locale). You can change it anytime from the language menu or with ?lang=fa, ?lang=tr, ?lang=en, ?lang=ru, or ?lang=ar.
