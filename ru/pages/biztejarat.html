About the project

Biztejarat was an SMM case study project — an industrial products Instagram page launched and managed by Bizdavar Group. This is a SMM management and B2B content case study, not a separate Bizdavar trading or business unit.

While active, electronic equipment, instrumentation, sensors and industrial parts (including VEGA, Prosense and related brands) were introduced and documented through posts and stories.
